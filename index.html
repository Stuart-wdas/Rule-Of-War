COMMANDER'S BRIEFING

COMMAND THE WAR IN FOUR STAGES.

01

DEPLOY

Spend supply to place buildings or recruit units. New assets arrive after the round resolves. Your commander can establish nine-tile, 3 × 3 buildings throughout their movement radius, or place a bridge by selecting its two riverbanks.

02

MOVE

Plot a connected destination at any distance. A small flag marks every round of travel; units advance one flag per round and cannot fire after moving. Bombers still complete one sortie.

03

ATTACK

Fire from the new positions. Multiple units may concentrate on one square. Units whose range touches a bomber's route automatically attempt to intercept it before payload release.

04

RESOLVE

Shots land together, reinforcements arrive, and the next round begins.
