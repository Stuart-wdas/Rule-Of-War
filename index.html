COMMANDER'S BRIEFING

COMMAND A LIVING BATTLEFIELD.

01

BUILD

Spend supply to place buildings or recruit units. New assets complete over time. Your commander can establish nine-tile, 3 × 3 buildings throughout their movement radius, or place a bridge by selecting its two riverbanks.

02

MOVE

Right-click a destination or use M. Units follow terrain-aware paths continuously, steer around formations, and repath when blocked.

03

ENGAGE

Units automatically acquire and fire on visible enemies. Right-click a hostile to focus it, or press A and designate an attack-move route.

04

CONTROL

Use S to stop, H to hold, Shift to queue commands, and Ctrl+1-9 to organize control groups. Solo play supports tactical pause.
